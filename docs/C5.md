# C5: daily long/short swing book

Spec: [`artifacts/models/c5.json`](../artifacts/models/c5.json). Code: `qforecast/swing/strategy.py`
(`book_targets`), `qforecast/swing/book.py` (`tranche_targets`).

## Rules

| Step | Rule |
|---|---|
| Universe | Top-40 USDT perps by 30-day quote volume, re-ranked on the 1st of each month, delisted coins included |
| Score | Weighted sum of 6 cross-sectional normal scores: low volatility, low beta, no lottery up-day (`max_7d`), low funding, close near the 90-day high, residual taker flow |
| Long leg | Top 5 by score, 0.5 gross, inverse 28-day volatility, at most 25 % of the leg per coin |
| Short leg | 8 names from the bottom, 0.5 gross. A candidate whose 7-day mean funding is negative is skipped and the next one is taken, down to rank 16 |
| Hedge | Legs scaled so that long beta = short beta (BTC, 90 days). With fewer than 4 eligible shorts the leg keeps 12.5 % per name and a BTC short carries the missing beta |
| Holding | 7 sub-books, one re-picks each day and holds 7 days: 1 / 7 of the book changes every day |
| Squeeze guard | A short whose daily close reaches 1.4 x its entry is bought back and stays out until its sub-book re-picks |
| Costs | 7 bps per side all-in, real funding every day |

## Why these rules

| Rule | Reason |
|---|---|
| Wider short leg than long leg | The losses of a cross-sectional crypto book come from single shorts that rally hard. More names per short leg means less weight on each one |
| Skip shorts with negative funding | Negative funding means shorts already pay longs: the short side is crowded and a squeeze is more likely |
| Staggered sub-books | One weekly rebalance makes the result depend on the weekday it started. Seven sub-books remove that luck and spread trading over the week |
| Beta hedge | The book should earn the spread between coins, not the market direction |

## Run it

```bash
python -m qforecast.swing.data                                  # build the daily cube (data/um/cube_daily_top40.npz)
python -m qforecast.swing.backtest --start 2025-01-01 --capital 1000 --exchange-minimum
```

The signal weights and leg sizes were chosen on the same 2021-2026 history the cube covers, so any backtest on it
is in-sample and overstates what to expect. Only data that the design never saw can confirm the strategy: paper
trading on live prices and a sealed forward window (`qforecast/vault.py`).
